# Build Guide:

### Step 1:
Get all the parts (Case top; case bottom; Soldered PCB; screws; Display: heated inserts).

### Step 2:
Insert the heated inserts into their places.

### Step 3:
Place the daughter boards and insert haptic actuator into its slot.
Place PCB on top of case bottom and screw it in place.

### Step 3:
Place case top on top of the PCB and case bottom and screw it in place.

### Step 4:
Connect the display with its connector and place it in its slot on case top.

### Step 5:
Plug the Pico in and flash the firmware.

### Step 6:
Attach all the keycaps and encoder caps.

### Step 7:
Done!!
